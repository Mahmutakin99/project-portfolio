Descriptions are original and assets belong to these projects. No reference project artwork, wording or popularity badges were copied.

## Assets and status

Featured images come from their corresponding showcase's documented real captures. PixelMend uses a NASA public-domain sample; SoulMate uses explicit preview fixtures. Older icons come from their source repositories. ChatLy's selected screen has empty login fields. Real conversations, member photographs and institutional operational data are excluded.

Previously licensed assets retain their permissions. Orbit's MIT and PixelMend's Apache-2.0 notices are preserved. This presentation does not revoke existing licenses.

Tests and builds cited in older case studies are historical records, not new executions. Appearance screenshots do not establish performance, model quality, release acceptance or a security audit.

## Coverage

Related SmartDoc Query prototypes share one product page. Small exercises are grouped in the learning catalogue. Nesto is a marked historical summary without current repository verification.

Upstream forks are not presented as original products. Public model records retain attribution and license information. Static privacy/support sites keep their existing purpose.
